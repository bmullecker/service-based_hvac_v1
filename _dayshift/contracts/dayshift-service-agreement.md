Search engine optimization is what determines whether your site shows up when someone searches for your services. A beautiful site with no SEO is like a billboard in the middle of a field — it exists, but nobody sees it.

| Package | Price |
|---|---|

| [ ] Full SEO Package | $2,000 |

**On-Page Basics** — Included with every build. Proper page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, and page speed optimization. The foundation every site needs.

**Local SEO Starter** — For businesses that serve a specific area. Includes Google Business Profile setup or optimization, plus 3 dedicated local landing pages targeting the towns and neighborhoods your customers are searching from (e.g., "pressure washing in Seven Valleys PA"). **Full SEO Package** — A comprehensive setup for businesses ready to compete seriously in search. Includes keyword research, full site SEO audit, 5+ local landing pages, and an ongoing optimization framework. Best paired with a content retainer for continued results.
