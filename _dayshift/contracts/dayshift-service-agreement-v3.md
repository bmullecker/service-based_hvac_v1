**Full Site Retainer — $125/mo or $1,250/yr**
Up to 2 hours/month. Covers:
- Text, copy, and photo updates across all pages
- Seasonal content updates within existing sections

**What website maintenance retainers do NOT include:**

Focused entirely on improving your search visibility. Completely separate from website maintenance.

- New local landing page creation as keyword opportunities arise (1 per quarter included)
- Monthly SEO report: rankings, visibility changes, competitor snapshot, next steps
- Quarterly 30-minute strategy review call

Best for: Businesses serious about generating leads through organic search.

**What the SEO Retainer does NOT include:**
- Website design or structural updates (covered by Website Maintenance Retainer)
- Paid advertising (Google Ads, Facebook Ads)
- Link building campaigns (available as a custom scope add-on)

## 15. Revisions & Change Requests

### Included Revisions
Two (2) revision rounds are included in the project price.

**One revision round = one consolidated email from the client containing all feedback for that round.**

**Major change (up to 3 per round):**
Full layout restructuring, color scheme changes, section reordering, hero redesign, significant content rewriting, addition or removal of major page sections, page type changes.

**Minor change (unlimited per round):**
Copy edits, grammar/spelling corrections, small spacing or sizing adjustments, color swaps within the existing palette, font size tweaks, phone number/address corrections.

**Gray area examples:**
- Swapping a hero image → major (it affects layout)
- Changing a headline → minor (it's copy)
- Moving a section to a different position on the page → major
- Adding a sentence to existing copy → minor

**Revision Timeline:** Each round completed within 3 business days of submission.

### Additional Revision Rounds

| Round | Price |
|---|---|
| 3rd and 4th rounds | $100/round |
| 5th round and beyond | $150/round or $85/hr (whichever is less) |

If a revision request requires more than 3 hours of work, you'll receive a cost estimate before we proceed.

### Submitting Revisions
All revision requests must be submitted in writing via email. Verbal requests are not binding. All feedback for a given round must be submitted in a single email to count as one round.

### Scope Changes
Adding pages, features, or integrations not in the original scope requires a written change order and revised quote before work begins. Scope changes may affect the launch date. _dayshift. does not charge for drafting change orders.

## 18. QA, Launch & Approval

### Pre-Launch QA Checklist
Before going live, _dayshift. verifies:

- [ ] All pages render correctly on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (desktop)
- [ ] All pages render correctly on iOS and Android mobile
- [ ] Contact form submits and delivers to client email
- [ ] All links work (no 404s or broken anchors)
- [ ] All images load at correct resolution
- [ ] Page speed score: Core Web Vitals passing
- [ ] SSL certificate active (HTTPS)
- [ ] Google Analytics + Search Console connected and receiving data
- [ ] Domain DNS propagated and resolving correctly
- [ ] Meta titles and descriptions set for all pages

### Client Approval
Before launch, you'll receive a staging link for final review and approval. You have **5 business days** to review and approve or submit final revisions. Approval may be given via email reply — no signature required for launch approval.

### Launch Definition
A website is "launched" when it is live on your domain, the QA checklist is complete, and you have given written approval (email is sufficient).

**Final payment is due within 3 business days of launch.**

### Post-Launch Grace Period
For **14 days** following launch, _dayshift. will fix critical bugs at no charge. Critical bugs include: broken contact form, missing pages, broken navigation, images failing to load, site down.

Non-critical issues (design tweaks, copy edits, new content) are handled via retainer or billed at $85/hr after launch.
