## 14. Revisions & Change Requests

**Included Revisions:**
Two (2) revision rounds are included in the project price. Each round = one consolidated submission of feedback from the client via email.

**What Counts as One Round:**
- Up to 3 major changes (layout restructuring, color schemes, section reordering, significant content rewriting, element additions/removals)
- Unlimited minor tweaks (copy edits, small spacing adjustments, color swaps within palette, font sizing, grammar)

**Timeline:** Each revision round completed within 3 business days of submission.

**Additional Revision Rounds:**
- **3rd and 4th rounds:** $100 per round (up to 3 major changes + unlimited minor tweaks)
- **5th round and beyond:** $150 per round or $85/hr, whichever is less

If a revision requires more than 3 hours of work, we'll notify you of the cost before proceeding.

**Revision Submission:** Must be in writing (email). All revisions for a given round submitted together count as one round.

## 18. Post-Launch Support & Launch Definition

**Launch Definition:**
A website is "launched" when it is live on your domain and you've approved the final QA checklist. Final payment is due within 3 business days of launch.

**14-Day Grace Period:**
For 14 days after launch, we'll fix any critical bugs at no charge (broken forms, links, missing images, layout issues). Feature requests, design tweaks, and non-critical issues are handled via retainer or hourly billing.

After the 14-day grace period, all changes are handled via retainer (if active) or billed hourly at $85/hr.

**Post-Launch (If No Retainer Selected):**
If you don't sign up for monthly hosting or retainer services, all post-launch support is your responsibility. We'll provide final files and offer one transfer assistance session to help you move to another provider. After that, updates, maintenance, hosting, and domain renewal are your responsibility. We're not liable for downtime, expired domains, or lapsed hosting without a retainer.
